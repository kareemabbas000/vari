## Rules for "top / best" list pages
- Include a company only with an **independent signal** (an industry award result, an editorial feature, a Wikipedia entry) and facts from its own site. Leave out anyone who can't be verified.
- **No numeric ranking**, and **VARI is never ranked**: disclose it in a separate "Where VARI fits" note.
- Show a "checked" date and refresh at least every 6 months. Dated pages ("2026") are updated each January, never re-dated without new content.
