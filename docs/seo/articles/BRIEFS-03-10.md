# Article briefs 03–10

Articles 01 and 02 are drafted in full. These eight are briefed to the same standard (everything except the final prose) because each one depends on facts only the VARI team has. Writing them without those facts would mean inventing experience, which is exactly what this strategy rules out. Each brief ends with **⟦VARI⟧ input**: once that arrives, each article is about a day of writing and editing.

Conventions for every brief:
- Titles ≤ 60 characters, descriptions ≤ 155.
- Slugs are per language.
- Schema: `BlogPosting` + `BreadcrumbList` + `FAQPage` unless stated otherwise.
- Images are VARI portfolio images where they add evidence, exported as JPEG/WebP ≤ 250 KB, with descriptive filenames.
- External links go to primary sources. Where demand evidence comes from Google autocomplete only, volume is **unverified**.
