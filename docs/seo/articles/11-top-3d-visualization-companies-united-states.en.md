# Top 3D Visualization Companies in the United States: A Sourced Guide for 2026

The American market for architectural visualization is enormous, and so is the noise around it. Search for the top 3D visualization companies in the US and you will find directory pages listing hundreds of firms, and "top ten" lists published by studios that rank themselves first. Neither tells you who the industry itself rates.

This guide uses a narrower rule. A company is included only if it has independent recognition, such as a CGArchitect 3D Awards result, a Wikipedia entry or established press coverage, and every fact comes from a named source.

## Studios with recent award recognition

**DBOX** presents itself as a strategic brand consultancy for design- and luxury-driven projects, with offices in Miami, New York, London, Budapest and Lviv. It won the Film category at the 2023 CGArchitect 3D Awards and Best Commissioned Film in 2018. It suits developers who want branding, film and imagery from one team.

**Brooklyn Digital Foundry** was named Viz Pro of the Year at the 2023 CGArchitect 3D Awards, one of the most competitive recognitions in the field.

**Neoscape** was established in Boston in 1995 as an architectural visualization firm and has grown into a full-service creative consultancy, with studios in Boston, New York and San Francisco according to a partner profile. Its interactive work was nominated at the 2023 CGArchitect 3D Awards.

**Kilograph** calls itself a global narrative strategy and creative agency, working on brand, interactive experiences, film and visualization for infrastructure, civic, institutional and commercial clients. It won the Interactive category at the 2018 CGArchitect 3D Awards.

## A younger New York studio with independent coverage

**Fortes.Vision** was founded in New York in 2017, according to its Wikipedia entry, and produces photorealistic renderings, animations, virtual tours and 360° panoramas for architects, developers and design agencies. The same entry notes that it was named Interior CGI Company USA and Architectural CGI Company USA in the 2025 International Property Awards.

## North American neighbours

Several US projects are visualized by studios across the border. **The Rapid Eye Movement** (Canada) won the interactive VR/AR category at the 2024 CGArchitect 3D Awards, and **PUREBLINK** (Canada) was nominated for interactive work in 2023. International studios also keep US bases: Germany's bloomimages, for example, lists an office in Brooklyn.

## What US projects need from a visualization studio

**Imperial drawings and US products.** American drawings are in feet and inches, and interiors are specified with US manufacturers. A studio should work comfortably in imperial units and with US product data.

**Marketing at US scale.** Off-plan condominium sales, multifamily leasing and commercial leasing campaigns need consistent series across brochures, websites, listing portals and sales galleries, often with film and interactive tools. That is where the campaign studios above are strongest.

**Budget.** Published 2026 price guides put a professional still at roughly US$600 to US$2,500 at most studios, with an average around US$1,500, and freelancers lower. Animation is priced per minute and costs far more. Our [rendering cost guide](/en/blog/architectural-rendering-cost) explains what moves the price.

**Time zones.** The continental US spans four time zones, and many firms work with studios abroad. A team several hours ahead can progress work while your office is closed, as long as review times are agreed. Our guide to [outsourcing architectural rendering](/en/blog/outsourcing-architectural-rendering) explains how to set that up.

## FAQ

**What are the top 3D visualization companies in the United States?**
Studios with independent recognition include DBOX (CGArchitect Film winner 2023), Brooklyn Digital Foundry (Viz Pro of the Year 2023), Neoscape (founded in Boston in 1995), Kilograph (Interactive winner 2018) and Fortes.Vision (New York, International Property Awards 2025).

**How much does a 3D rendering cost in the US?**
Published 2026 guides put a professional still image at about US$600 to US$2,500 at most studios. Prices depend on modelling effort, number of views, realism and turnaround.

**Should a US developer hire a studio abroad?**
Many do, for capacity and price. It works well when the studio handles imperial drawings and US products, communicates fluently in English and agrees review times that fit your schedule.

**Where are the recognised US visualization studios based?**
Among the studios in this guide, New York comes up most often, followed by Boston, San Francisco and Miami.
