# The Best 3D Rendering Companies and Visualization Studios in 2026, According to the Industry's Own Awards

Every January a new crop of "best rendering companies" lists appears, and most of them share a quiet flaw: they are written by a rendering company, which tends to finish first. For 2026 we took a different route. Instead of our opinion, we looked at the one verdict the visualization industry gives itself every year, the CGArchitect 3D Awards, now presented with Chaos at the World Visualization Festival, and asked what the last three editions tell a buyer.

The short answer: the best 3D rendering companies right now are spread across far more countries than the old London-New York axis, and the work that wins is increasingly moving, interactive or both.

## The 2025 winners

The 21st edition was presented at the World Visualization Festival in Warsaw in October 2025. The category winners were:

- **Studio of the Year: SALTVISION** (Belgium), also nominated for interactive work.
- **Commissioned Film: Arqui9 Visualisation** (United Kingdom).
- **Commissioned Image: RYTM STUDIO** (Ukraine).
- **Interactive VR/AR Experience: Kinerama and Bwow Studio** (Spain).
- **Non-Commissioned Film: Kunkun Visual** (Indonesia).
- **Non-Commissioned Image: Mátyás Kovács** (Hungary).
- **Viz Pro of the Year: Stefana Ristić** (Serbia).

Among the nominees for Studio of the Year were **Yellow Studio** (Estonia) and **Nightnurse Images** (Switzerland). **Okdraw** (Portugal) was nominated for both image and film, and **Brick Visual** (Hungary) for film and interactive work.

## The 2024 and 2023 results

In 2024, **Brick Visual** won Studio of the Year and Best Commissioned Image. **Tecma Solutions** (Italy) won Commissioned Film, and **The Rapid Eye Movement** (Canada) the interactive category. Viz Pro of the Year went to **Mohamed Ali Salih** from Egypt.

In 2023, **DBOX** (United States) won Film, **Brooklyn Digital Foundry** (United States) was Viz Pro of the Year, and **Kinerama** (Spain) won Interactive. **Neoscape** (United States) and **Recent Spaces** (United Kingdom) were among the nominees.

## What three years of results tell a buyer

**Excellence is no longer concentrated in a few cities.** Recent winners and nominees come from Belgium, Hungary, Ukraine, Spain, Italy, Portugal, Estonia, Serbia, Indonesia, Egypt, Canada and the United States. For a developer or architect, that means you can find award-level quality in a time zone and price range that suits you, rather than defaulting to the most famous names.

**Moving and interactive work is gaining ground.** Separate categories for commissioned film and interactive VR/AR experiences, and repeat winners such as Kinerama, show how much of the best work is now experienced rather than looked at. If your project is a sales launch, it is worth asking a studio about film and real-time work, not only stills. Our comparison of [rendering, animation and virtual tours](/en/blog/3d-rendering-vs-animation-vs-virtual-tour) helps decide which you need.

**Consistency counts.** Brick Visual appears in winner or nominee lists across several recent editions. A single brilliant image can be a fluke; a studio that is recognised year after year has a reliable process behind it.

**Awards reward ambition, not always fit.** An award-winning commissioned film was made for a specific client, budget and brief. It shows what a studio can do at its best, which is not the same as what it will do on a modest budget in a tight schedule.

## How to use award lists without being misled

Start with the categories that match your need: commissioned work, not personal projects, if you are hiring for a real launch. Then look at each studio's full portfolio, not just the winning piece, and ask for a complete series from one project. Our [checklist for choosing a 3D rendering studio](/en/blog/how-to-choose-a-3d-rendering-studio) covers the rest, and our evergreen guide to [the best architectural visualization studios](/en/blog/best-architectural-visualization-studios) profiles the names recognised over the longer term.

Budget matters too. Studios at this level usually price accordingly; our [rendering cost guide](/en/blog/architectural-rendering-cost) explains what moves the price per image.

## FAQ

**Which are the best 3D rendering companies in 2026?**
Going by the most recent industry awards, studios such as SALTVISION (Studio of the Year 2025), Brick Visual (Studio of the Year 2024), Arqui9 and RYTM Studio (2025 commissioned film and image) are among the best-recognised. The best company for your project still depends on its type, budget and time zone.

**What are the best visualization studios in 2026?**
There is no official ranking, but the CGArchitect 3D Awards are the most widely followed independent signal. Their 2024 and 2025 Studio of the Year winners were Brick Visual and SALTVISION.

**What are the CGArchitect 3D Awards?**
An annual international award for architectural visualization, established in 2004 and now presented with Chaos at the World Visualization Festival. Categories cover commissioned and personal images and films, interactive experiences, Studio of the Year and Viz Pro of the Year.

**Should I only hire award-winning studios?**
Not necessarily. Awards show a studio's best work under good conditions. For many projects, a studio whose portfolio matches your building type, budget and working hours will deliver better value.
