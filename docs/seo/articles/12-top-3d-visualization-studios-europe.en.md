# Top 3D Visualization Studios in Europe, Country by Country

Europe is where much of the world's most admired architectural visualization is made. It is also where the map has shifted most in recent years: some of the studios winning the industry's top awards are now in Budapest, Kyiv, Antwerp or Lisbon, not only in London and Paris.

To keep this guide useful, we included a studio only if it has an independent signal, either a win or nomination at the CGArchitect 3D Awards between 2023 and 2025 or a feature in ArchDaily. For Germany, Switzerland and Austria, see our separate guide to the [top 3D visualization companies in Germany](/en/blog/top-3d-visualization-companies-germany).

## Belgium

**SALTVISION** won Studio of the Year at the 2025 CGArchitect 3D Awards and was also nominated for interactive VR/AR work the same year. It is the most recent holder of the field's top studio title.

## Hungary

**Brick Visual** (Budapest, with offices in Cluj-Napoca and Porto) won Studio of the Year and Best Commissioned Image in 2024 and was nominated again in 2025 for commissioned film and interactive work. It describes itself as a creative and technology partner for real estate and global brands, covering stills, film, interactive sales tools and project branding.

## United Kingdom

**Arqui9 Visualisation** won Best Commissioned Film at the 2025 awards. **Recent Spaces** won Best Commissioned Image in 2018 and was nominated for film in 2023. **Eleven Visualisation** was nominated in the Film category in 2023, and **Inferstudio** for Best Commissioned Film in 2025. **Hayes Davidson** in London, founded in 1989, was described by ArchDaily as one of the longest-running visualization studios and the first of its kind in the UK.

## Ukraine

**RYTM STUDIO** won Best Commissioned Image at the 2025 CGArchitect 3D Awards, one of the most competitive categories.

## Italy

**Tecma Solutions** won Best Commissioned Film at the 2024 awards.

## Spain

**Kinerama** won the Interactive category in 2023 and, with **Bwow Studio**, the Interactive VR/AR Experience category in 2025. That makes Spain a strong choice for real-time and VR sales experiences.

## Portugal

**Okdraw** was nominated in the Image category in 2023 and, in 2025, for both Best Commissioned Image and Best Non-Commissioned Film, a sign of consistent quality across formats.

## Estonia

**Yellow Studio** was one of the nominees for Studio of the Year in 2025.

## Norway and France

**MIR** (Norway), founded in 2000, and **Luxigon** (Paris) were both featured by ArchDaily among distinguished visualization studios. MIR is known for atmospheric, landscape-led imagery, and Luxigon for bold competition imagery. We profile both in our evergreen guide to [the best architectural visualization studios](/en/blog/best-architectural-visualization-studios).

## What the European map tells a buyer

**Award-level quality is available across the continent.** You no longer need to hire in a capital city to get recognised work. Several of the most decorated recent studios are in Central and Eastern Europe.

**Specialities differ.** Film has been a strength of UK and Italian winners, interactive work of Spanish ones, and the Studio of the Year titles went to Hungary and Belgium. Match the studio to the format you need. Our comparison of [rendering, animation and virtual tours](/en/blog/3d-rendering-vs-animation-vs-virtual-tour) helps decide.

**Cross-border projects are normal.** Most of Europe shares one or two hours of time difference, and English is the working language of the industry. Still, check in writing which language reviews happen in, how many revision rounds are included and who owns the images.

## FAQ

**What are the top 3D visualization studios in Europe?**
Recent award results point to SALTVISION (Belgium, Studio of the Year 2025), Brick Visual (Hungary, Studio of the Year 2024), Arqui9 (UK) and RYTM Studio (Ukraine). MIR (Norway), Hayes Davidson (UK) and Luxigon (France) are long-established names recognised in the architecture press.

**Which European country is best for architectural visualization?**
There is no single answer. The UK has the longest-established studios, Hungary and Belgium hold the latest Studio of the Year titles, and Spain is strong in interactive work.

**Is it safe to hire a visualization studio in another European country?**
Yes, with a clear contract: scope, revision rounds, deadlines, usage rights and confidentiality agreed in writing before the project starts.

**Where is the World Visualization Festival held?**
The 2025 edition, where the CGArchitect 3D Awards were presented, took place in Warsaw, Poland.
